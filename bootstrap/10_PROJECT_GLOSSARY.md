# Journey Planner Project Glossary

Version: 1.0

Status: Active

---

# Purpose

This glossary defines the official vocabulary used throughout the Journey Planner project.

Its purpose is to ensure every contributor—human or AI—uses the same terminology when discussing the product, writing documentation, designing interfaces, implementing features, or communicating with users.

Consistent language creates consistent products.

---

# Philosophy

Every important concept should have one official name.

Avoid using multiple words for the same concept.

When a new term is introduced, it should first be defined here before becoming part of the product language.

---

# Product Terms

## Journey

The complete travel plan created by an Explorer.

A Journey contains one or more Journey Days.

A Journey may exist without any bookings.

---

## Journey Day

One calendar day within a Journey.

Each Journey Day contains one or more Itinerary Items.

---

## Itinerary Item

A planned activity within a Journey Day.

Examples:

- Homestay
- Coffee Workshop
- Forest Walk
- Meditation Session
- Tea Plantation Visit

An Itinerary Item may later become a Booking.

---

## Experience

A verified destination, activity, service, or place that can be added to a Journey.

Experience is the official product term.

It is the primary content object within the platform.

Examples:

- Homestay
- Workshop
- Guided Walk
- Restaurant
- Farm
- Waterfall

---

## Booking

A confirmed reservation attached to an Itinerary Item.

Booking supports the Journey.

Booking is never the primary product.

---

## Availability

The published schedule that determines whether an Experience can be booked.

Availability does not guarantee a Booking.

It only indicates possible reservation opportunities.

---

# User Roles

## Explorer

The customer.

Creates Journeys.

Discovers Experiences.

Plans travel.

Makes Bookings.

Learns through the platform.

---

## Scout

Internal platform operator.

Collects data.

Verifies Experiences.

Maintains quality.

Publishes information.

Protects accuracy.

---

## Host

Future role.

Provides Experiences.

Maintains availability.

Manages reservations.

Version 1 intentionally excludes Host Portal functionality.

---

## Administrator

Future platform management role.

Responsible for operational oversight.

# Creative Terms

## Creative Framework

The shared artistic foundation inherited from Nature Base Workspace.

Includes:

- Pixel Language
- Master Palette
- Art Direction
- Companion
- Animation
- Asset Production Pipeline

The Creative Framework is shared across products.

---

## Pixel Language

The highest authority for pixel-art production.

Defines the visual grammar of the studio.

---

## Master Palette

The official colour palette used by every production asset.

Only one Master Palette exists.

---

## Companion

The calm digital guide shared across products.

The Companion observes.

The Companion never commands.

---

## World Engine

The shared environmental philosophy used across projects.

Defines atmosphere, transitions, movement, lighting, and environmental consistency.

# Workflow Terms

## Discovery

Understand the problem.

Define philosophy.

No implementation.

---

## Lock

Explicit approval.

The decision becomes permanent until intentionally reopened.

---

## Production

Transform approved philosophy into implementation-ready decisions.

---

## Implementation

Engineering execution.

No redesign.

---

## Verification

Technical verification plus human QA.

Both are required.

---

## Release

Stable milestone.

Verified.

Documented.

Committed.

Pushed.

# Naming Philosophy

Internal engineering names should remain descriptive.

User-facing names may be more expressive where appropriate.

Example

Internal:

Experience

User Interface:

Coffee Experience

Forest Experience

Tea Experience

Future interface language may evolve while preserving internal terminology.

---

# Guiding Principle

One concept.

One official name.

Every contributor speaks the same language.

---

# Amendment Rule

Changes to the Glossary require explicit locked approval.

New terminology should be added here before becoming part of the project's official language.
